Executive Profile

About Dr. Shubhangi Patil-Solanki

Bridging theoretical computer science and cutting-edge deep learning systems

Dr. Shubhangi R. Patil-Solanki is a passionate academician, researcher, and educator who has spent over 23 years mentoring undergraduate and graduate students, driving curriculum modernization, and contributing to institutional excellence. She currently serves as an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at the School of Engineering & Technology, PCET's Pimpri Chinchwad University (PCU), Pune.

Prior to joining PCU, she served for more than a decade as Head of the Department (HOD) and Associate Professor in Computer Engineering at Sinhgad Institute of Technology (SIT), Lonavala. Throughout her career, she has held key administrative responsibilities including Department NBA & NAAC Accreditation Coordinator, University Staff Selection Committee Member at Mumbai University, and Subject Chairman at North Maharashtra University.

Her research focus centers on Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning architectures, and Medical Image Processing, specifically fractional-order variational CNNs for image denoising and automated MRI brain tumor classification.
